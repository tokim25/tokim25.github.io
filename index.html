Career

How I got here, one role at a time.

I care about helping people become the best version of themselves. Facilitation showed me that happening in a room. Training made it repeatable. Instructional design built it in before the room even started. Leading a team changed the question from “did this land” to “will it hold up when I'm not there,” and that's what pulled me toward Learning Architect: building systems that put the right education at the right place at the right time.
